What are AI Agents and how can AISI help create them to improve business operations?

AI agents are intelligent systems that can automate tasks, make contextual decisions, and interact with enterprise software platforms. They are not simply rule-based bots that follow a certain sequence but they are able to interpret your intent, make decisions and execute actions to help complete workflows.

Our AI agents can be used for:

  • Workflow automation and task orchestration
  • Customer service automation
  • Document review and summarisation
  • Sales lead qualification
  • Compliance checks and reporting
  • Internal knowledge assistants

Unlike simple chatbots, AI agents integrate with your systems to perform actions and support decision-making processes.

07

What is Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)?

Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) uses artificial intelligence to read, extract, interpret, and process data from structured and unstructured documents like manual forms and documents, PDFs or even images. Unlike traditional OCR software, AI-powered IDP understands context and supports automated workflows. Common use cases include:

  • Invoice processing and accounts payable automation
  • Contract analysis
  • Insurance claims processing
  • KYC and onboarding documentation
  • Regulatory compliance documentation

Our IDP solutions help organisations reduce manual processing and improve accuracy as part of an AI Agent's workflow.

Do we need a large amount of data to implement AI?

Although AI solutions are dependent on having data, it is not necessarily so as there are various existing data sets or models that we can adopt to begin your AI solution development. While having historical data does indeed reduce the time to achieve ideal model performance, many AI solutions can be implemented using:

  • Hybrid rule-based and AI approaches
  • Pilot deployments
  • Workflow-driven automation
  • External benchmarking models

Our AI specialists can help you determine your company's AI Readiness stage and we can work with you to design AI solutions suited to your organisation's data maturity level.
