04 / In practice

Four stages, in order

Forward deployed, from first map to run mode

Most AI work fails between the demo and the deployment. Our delivery model is forward deployed engineering (FDE) — an engineer embedded in your operation for the duration, working from your real data and your real constraints rather than a specification written three months earlier.

  1. Map

    We start with what you already have running — the systems, the spreadsheets, the workarounds, the AI tools individual teams bought themselves.

  2. Prioritise

    Not everything that can be automated should be. We rank candidates by operational impact against build cost, and tell you which ones aren't worth doing.

  3. Build

    An engineer embedded with your team builds against live data from week one, starting from components already tested on previous deployments.

  4. Integrate & hand over

    The system goes into your environment — cloud, on-premise or hybrid — documented and yours. From there we move into run mode: monitoring, updates and cost optimisation.

Based in four cities, deployed across the region

Forward deployed engineering means being close to the work. Our engineers are based in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Ho Chi Minh City and Shanghai, and deploy from those four cities to wherever the client’s operation is — on site, in the team, for as long as the build takes.

Six more cities are on the roadmap through 2027.

Your decision, written down before we build

Where your data lives is your decision. We deploy cloud, on-premise or hybrid to your requirement, and we’ll put that in writing before anything is built.
