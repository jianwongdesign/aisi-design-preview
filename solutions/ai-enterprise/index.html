AI Solutions for the Retail Industry

AI helps retailers optimise operations, understand customer behaviour, and improve store performance.

  • Intelligent store operations monitoring

  • Precision marketing and customer targeting

  • Demand forecasting and inventory optimisation

  • Product feedback and sentiment analysis

Intelligent Store Operations

What it does

AI is used to continuously monitor and evaluate store operations by analysing visual data (from cameras, images, or mobile uploads) and operational data to detect issues.

How it works

Computer vision models recognise shelves, products, equipment, and store layouts, while rule engines and workflows convert detections into actionable tasks.

Business Value

Replacing manual store inspections, ensuring merchandising consistency across outlets, reducing labour costs, improving compliance, and increasing sales through better in-store execution.

AI Solutions for Financial Services

AI enables financial institutions to improve risk management, operational efficiency, and customer engagement.

  • Intelligent marketing and product recommendations

  • Fraud detection and AML monitoring

  • Automated document processing

  • Customer service and call analytics

Intelligent Marketing

What it does

AI identifies which customers are most likely to buy which financial products and recommends personalised offers through effective channels.

How it works

Predictive models analyse transaction patterns, customer profiles, and behavioural data to estimate conversion probability and expected value.

Business Value

Boosting campaign response rates, increasing transaction volumes, improving wealth product uptake, and maximising customer lifetime value.
