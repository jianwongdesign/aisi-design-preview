01 / The context

Traditional Video Monitoring Is Passive

The Problem

Most organisations already have extensive camera infrastructure. But these systems rely heavily on manual monitoring and human observation, which leads to missed incidents and inefficient resource allocation.

  1. Missed safety hazards

  2. Delayed incident response

  3. High operational monitoring costs

  4. Limited ability to analyse historical video data

02 / The approach

AI-Powered Video Intelligence

AISI deploys advanced computer vision models that continuously analyse video streams — identifying patterns, anomalies, and risks in real time, automatically.

  1. Detect safety violations

  2. Identify operational anomalies

  3. Monitor compliance conditions

  4. Generate automated incident reports

  5. Trigger alerts and workflows

Your cameras become intelligent operational sensors.

03 / In practice

Core Capabilities

Real-Time Hazard Detection

Automatically identify unsafe conditions such as missing guardrails, workers without PPE, restricted zone violations, or equipment misuse.

Automated Compliance Documentation

Generate incident summaries and regulatory reports automatically based on detected events.

Custom Behaviour Models

Go beyond off-the-shelf detection to model custom behaviours specific to your environment and operational needs.

Intelligent Event Alerts

Instant notifications delivered to platforms like Microsoft Teams or operational dashboards when potential risks are detected.

04 / In practice

AI Video Analytics Workflow

  1. On-Site Capture

    Existing CCTV cameras capture live video feeds from operational environments.

  2. AI Vision Analysis

    Computer vision models analyse video streams to detect hazards, anomalies, or defined conditions.

  3. Human-in-the-Loop Review

    Detected events are presented to supervisors through interactive alerts for confirmation.

  4. Automated Reporting & Workflow Integration

    Confirmed incidents trigger automated reports, alerts, and compliance documentation.

05 / In practice

Where AI Video Analytics Delivers Impact

Use Cases

  1. Construction Safety Monitoring

    Detect missing safety barriers, unsafe worker behaviour, and potential fall hazards in real time.

  2. Smart Facility Management

    Monitor restricted zones, unauthorised access, and operational anomalies.

  3. Workplace Safety Compliance

    Automate reporting of safety observations and regulatory compliance incidents.

  4. Operational Efficiency Monitoring

    Identify workflow bottlenecks and operational inefficiencies across facilities.
