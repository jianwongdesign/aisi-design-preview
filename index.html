Our engineers work from inside your operation.

Forward deployed engineering (FDE) means our engineers work from inside your operation rather than from a specification. They sit with your team, go through the daily work task by task, and see the data as it actually arrives — incomplete, inconsistent, spread across systems that were never meant to talk to each other. That is where most AI projects quietly fail, and it is not visible from a requirements document.

Working this way means the gaps get found and closed during the build rather than discovered at go-live. The system is shaped by how the work is actually done, not by how it was described in a meeting.

Forward deployed only works if someone can be there.

Our engineers are based in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Ho Chi Minh City and Shanghai, and deploy from those four cities to clients across the region.

  1. Side by side with your team

    We work through the daily tasks with the people who do them, including the exceptions and workarounds nobody wrote down.

  2. Your data from day one

    The first working version runs against live inputs, not a cleaned sample set.

  3. Bridging the data gaps

    Missing fields, inconsistent formats, systems that don't connect — we close these as part of the build, not as a later phase.
