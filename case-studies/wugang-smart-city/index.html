01 / The context

A Growing City, Outpacing Manual Management

Wugang City, spanning 640 square kilometres with a population of over 320,000, faced the massive logistical challenge of modernising its urban management. With limited manpower and a sprawling geography, the city's existing surveillance infrastructure was severely underutilised.

  1. Operational Inefficiency

    Manual street patrols were slow, costly, and often missed illegal activities like unauthorised garbage disposal or road obstructions in high-density areas.

  2. Passive Monitoring

    Surveillance was primarily used for "post-incident" review. With over 2,100 cameras, it was physically impossible for human teams to monitor live feeds for real-time issues.

  3. Decentralised Data Silos

    Different departments (traffic, public safety, infrastructure) operated independent camera setups, making coordinated responses to city-wide incidents nearly impossible.

02 / The approach

One Unified Smart City Command Centre

AISI helped transform Wugang into a Smart City by integrating over 2,100 cameras into a single consolidated platform — leveraging advanced AI Video Analytics to provide 24/7 automated city-wide oversight.

  1. Automated Incident Detection

    Gain valuable insights into various aspects such as traffic patterns, pedestrian movement, and public gatherings.

  2. Critical Infrastructure Protection

    Visual sensors and video surveillance monitor power substations and government offices, triggering immediate alerts for any unauthorised intrusions or potential security threats.

  3. Mobile Data Intelligence

    To supplement fixed cameras, patrol vehicles were equipped with GPS and AI cameras, providing real-time data collection in high-population zones.

  4. Smart Dispatch System

    Central hub connecting all platforms to form a unified management platform. AI-generated events are transmitted for automatic processing and manual review for the dispatch of law enforcement personnel — ensuring a fully controlled and audited process.

  5. Instant Alerts on Incidents

    Real-time AI analysis of camera video feeds to auto-detect and generate event alerts of:

    • Illegal parking

    • Illegal garbage disposal

    • Obstruction of road

    • Roadside stalls

03 / In practice

City-Wide Impact at Scale

Unprecedented Scale

Successfully connected and managed over 2,100 CCTVs into one unified management interface.

Proven Responsiveness

18,273 out of 18,495 reported city matters addressed as of 2023 — demonstrating a near-perfect resolution rate.

Actionable Intelligence

The platform generated over 35,000 automated event alerts as of 2024, significantly reducing the need for manual patrolling.

Operational Excellence

Full data traceability achieved and inter-departmental cooperation improved while lowering the overall cost of city operations.
