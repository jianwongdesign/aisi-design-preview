01 / The context

Field Observations Stuck in Administrative Bottlenecks

For safety teams on active construction sites, the transition from field observation to formal documentation is often a bottleneck. The client faced several key challenges in their manual reporting process.

  1. Inconsistent Reporting

    The quality and detail of reports varied significantly based on the individual experience of the inspector.

  2. Regulatory Complexity

    Inspectors had to manually reference and check observations against complex WSH and BCA regulations.

  3. Administrative Burden

    Manually creating safety inspection reports after site visits was time-consuming and tedious for the safety team.

02 / The approach

From Site Photo to Audit-Ready Report in Minutes

AISI developed a custom AI Inspection Reporting Agent that transforms raw site observations into audit-ready documents easily, quickly and seamlessly. By leveraging advanced Vision AI and natural language processing, the solution streamlines the entire workflow:

  1. Seamless Data Capture

    Inspectors capture photos on-site and upload them via Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp, accompanied by short text notes or voice memos.

  2. AI Vision & Regulatory Analysis

    The agent utilises vision models to analyse images, identifies risks (e.g., fall hazards or missing guardrails), and automatically maps them to specific violated regulations, such as WSH (Work at Heights) Regulations 2014.

  3. Automated Report Generation

    The AI structures findings into a standardised template, including risk levels, observation summaries, and required immediate actions.

  4. Integrated Workflow

    After a quick review and confirmation on Teams, the final report is automatically logged on SharePoint and distributed to relevant email groups or Teams channels.

03 / In practice

Shifting Focus from Paperwork to Prevention

Instant Reporting

Eliminated the "admin lag" by allowing reports to be generated and filed before the inspector even leaves the site.

Regulatory Precision

Every observation is backed by specific BCA and WSH citations, ensuring high-standard compliance and reduced dependence on individual inspector memory.

Standardised Documentation

Achieved 100% consistency in report formatting and quality across all company projects and sites.

Audit Readiness

Improved traceability with a clear digital paper trail of photos, timestamps, and confirmed actions for every single safety observation recorded.
