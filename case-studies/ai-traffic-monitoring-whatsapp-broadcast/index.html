Automating 24/7 Public Traffic Reporting with an Autonomous AI Agent

01 / The context

Manual Monitoring, Round the Clock

A major multi-year roadworks and flyover programme placed sustained pressure on surrounding traffic. To keep the public informed, the responsible agency needed structured condition updates broadcast at fixed intervals every day, without exception — a requirement that quickly outgrew what a manual process could sustainably deliver.

  1. Continuous Coverage, No Days Off

    Updates were required at multiple fixed timings daily, 365 days a year — a workload with no natural downtime, regardless of weekends, holidays, or staff availability.

  2. Manual CCTV Review at Scale

    Each update required an engineer to individually review live camera feeds across several junctions, interpret congestion levels, and manually draft a structured summary before every broadcast.

  3. High-Value Engineering Time Diverted

    Skilled engineering staff were tied up on a repetitive monitoring task rather than higher-value project work — a recurring, low-leverage use of specialist time.

02 / The approach

An AI Agent Built to Watch, Analyse, and Report

AISI deployed an AI agent that connects directly to the existing CCTV infrastructure, interprets traffic conditions using AI video analytics, and generates structured public updates on a fixed schedule — with no manual review step required under normal conditions.

  1. Automated Video Analysis

    The agent continuously reads live camera feeds at key junctions, classifying congestion levels — light, heavy, queue length — without human interpretation.

  2. Multi-Route Condition Aggregation

    Conditions across multiple routes and junctions are aggregated into a single, consistent report format, ready for public broadcast every reporting cycle.

  3. Scheduled Broadcast Generation

    At each pre-set reporting time, the agent compiles the structured update — incident status, weather, per-route conditions, and supporting camera stills — and pushes it directly to the public WhatsApp broadcast channel.

  4. Human-in-the-Loop Escalation

    If the agent detects a major jam or anomalous event outside normal patterns, it routes an alert to the operations team for confirmation before the update is released.

The AI Agent slots seamlessly into the existing reporting workflow, without disrupting operations or requiring staff to learn new systems or processes.

03 / In practice

From Manual Task to Autonomous Workflow

~1,916 Hours Saved Annually

Eliminated the need for staff to manually monitor CCTV feeds and draft updates around the clock, 365 days a year.

~$91,800 in Annual Cost Savings

Direct labour-cost reduction from removing a full-time-equivalent manual monitoring workload.

Consistent, Reliable Reporting

Every scheduled update is now delivered on time, every day, without dependency on staff availability or shift coverage.

Faster Escalation on Major Incidents

Anomalous conditions are flagged to the operations team in near real-time, rather than surfacing only at the next scheduled manual check.
