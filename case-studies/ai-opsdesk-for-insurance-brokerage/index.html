AI OpsDesk: Running Enquiry-to-Renewal on One Case Record

A Singapore brokerage handling up to 2,000 enquiries a month across seven product lines — every case run by hand, and each product line’s workflow resting on just one or two staff.

ClientSingapore Insurance Brokerage
IndustryInsurance brokerage
CapabilityAI agents & automation

On this page

01The Challenge02The Solution03What Was BuiltStart a conversation ↗

01 / The context

The Challenge

  1. Manual document handling at volume

    Policy documents arrived as PDFs and messaging-app images. Each one was read by a person and re-keyed into Excel, insurer portals and the broking system — the same data entered three or four times.

  2. Key-person dependency per product line

    Each product line ran on one or two people holding the working knowledge of how a case moved from enquiry to bound policy. Leave, illness or resignation was an unmanaged business risk.

  3. No single case record

    Enquiries arrived by email, messaging app and web form with no shared queue, so the status of any case was impossible to see without asking someone.

02 / The approach

The Solution

An AI-driven enquiry-to-renewal foundation, built on a single high-volume product line first and designed so every other line reuses the same spine.

01 · One case queue

Email, messaging-app and web enquiries merge into a single case record that every downstream form reads from.

02 · Vision-based extraction

Corporate filings, approval letters, identity documents and financial statements are read automatically, with the system chasing whatever is missing rather than waiting.

03 · Drafted, not sent

Insurer RFQs and client quotes are drafted by the agent and insurer replies classified on arrival — staff approve every outbound message.

04 · Auto-fill and file

Insurer templates, portals and the broking system are populated from the one case record; documents file automatically and renewal emails draft on schedule.

Enquiry to renewal, in 12 stages

Dark stages run automatically. Light stages are AI-drafted and staff-approved.

  1. Enquiry intake

  2. Document extraction

  3. First-pass screening

  4. Insurer RFQs

  5. Quotation handling

  6. Client confirmation

  7. Insurer-specific filing

  8. Regulatory clearance

  9. Policy creation

  10. Delivery & payment

  11. Document storage

  12. Renewals
