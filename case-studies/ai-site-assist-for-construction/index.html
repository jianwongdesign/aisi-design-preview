AI Site Assist: Running Multi-Site Construction Ops from One Chat

Daily reports on paper, equipment tracked in scattered chats, service dates held in someone’s head — and head office with no reliable view of any of it.

ClientSingapore Construction Contractor
IndustryConstruction
CapabilityAI agents & automation

On this page

01The Challenge02The Solution03What Was BuiltStart a conversation ↗

01 / The context

The Challenge

  1. Paper and scattered chats

    Daily reports, delivery orders, diesel and scrap loads lived across group chats and hardcopy, spread over multiple concurrent projects with nothing consolidated.

  2. Servicing tracked by hand

    Machine meter readings were recorded manually, so overdue servicing surfaced late — usually after the machine had already run past its due point.

  3. No head-office visibility

    HQ could not see which sites had filed, where equipment physically sat, or what had actually happened on any given site that day.

02 / The approach

The Solution

One AI agent living inside the chat app the site teams already use. Text, voice note or photo — no new app, no training programme.

01 · Filing by conversation

Daily reports with delivery orders, diesel and scrap loads are filed in plain language; photos file themselves to the document store.

02 · Servicing that flags itself

Every meter reading updates service status, and overdue machines are flagged to managers the moment the reading lands.

03 · Equipment in one view

Book, withdraw and return equipment with one-tap approval, with live counts across every site rather than a spreadsheet updated weekly.

04 · Ask in plain English

Questions across every project answered in the chat, plus a daily digest, overdue alerts and on-demand PDF or Excel reports.
