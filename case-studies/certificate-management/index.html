01 / The context

Manual Compliance Creating Operational Risk

In the fast-paced construction industry, maintaining and tracking valid certifications for heavy machinery and lifting equipment is critical for both safety and legal compliance. The client faced significant operational bottlenecks due to entirely manual administrative processes.

  1. Operational Downtime

    Lapsed certifications resulted in urgent last-minute renewals or, in worst-case scenarios, total work stoppages on-site.

  2. High Risk of Oversight

    Manual entry was tedious and prone to human error, potentially leading to missed expiry dates and compliance failures.

  3. Manual Tracking Burdens

    The team was manually checking and updating validity dates for LG/LM certificates and other essential machinery documentation across all sites.

02 / The approach

An AI Agent Built Into Your Existing Workflow

AISI developed a custom AI Certificate Management Agent integrated with Microsoft 365 (Teams, SharePoint, Email) and WhatsApp — creating a seamless, zero-friction compliance workflow that slots directly into the site team's daily operations.

  1. Intelligent Document Ingestion

    Users simply capture a photo or upload a PDF of a certificate via Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp — no new systems or processes to learn.

  2. AI-Powered OCR Extraction

    Using AI Document Intelligence, the agent automatically extracts key data points — Certificate Type, Serial Number, and Expiry Date — with high-confidence text recognition.

  3. Automated Database Integration

    Once confirmed by the user, the agent automatically updates the centralised Excel tracking sheet on SharePoint, creating new entries or extending existing ones without manual typing.

  4. Proactive Expiry Alerts

    The system tracks remaining days and automatically triggers multi-stage alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiry via Teams or WhatsApp to ensure timely renewals.

The AI Agent slots seamlessly into the site team's existing daily workflow, without disrupting operations or requiring them to learn new systems or processes.

03 / In practice

From Reactive Admin to Proactive Compliance

Reduced Work Stoppages

By eliminating "missed" renewals, the client effectively removed the risk of downtime caused by expired equipment certifications.

Enhanced Accuracy

AI-powered extraction significantly reduced human data-entry errors, ensuring the integrity of safety records across all sites.

Scalable Compliance

The solution handles a wide array of certificate types and is standardised across all company sites without additional overhead.

Improved Reliability

Personnel now receive timely notifications and reminders, allowing them to focus on site operations rather than administrative paperwork.
